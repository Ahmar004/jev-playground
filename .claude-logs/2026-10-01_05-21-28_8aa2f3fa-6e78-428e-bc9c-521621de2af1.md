No, that's not the DSN. The Security Token is a different setting, and you can ignore it.

To find the DSN:

1. In Sentry, click **Settings** (gear icon, left sidebar), then **Projects**, then **jevs-playground**.
2. In the project's settings menu, under **SDK Setup**, click **Client Keys (DSN)**.
3. Copy the value labelled **DSN**. It looks like this (made-up values):
   `https://abc123def456abc123def456@o1234567.ingest.us.sentry.io/7654321`

Check that you have the right one:

- It starts with `https://`.
- It contains `@o` followed by numbers, and `ingest`.
- It ends with `/` followed by numbers.

Put it on the `NEXT_PUBLIC_SENTRY_DSN` line in `.env.local`.

The DSN is safe in the browser; it only lets the app send errors to your project. Still, type it straight into `.env.local` and not into this chat.
