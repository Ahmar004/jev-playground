I can't promise the app is completely bug free, but here is everything I found and fixed:

- **Pages were slow under load.** The first 1,000-user test served only 33 pages a second, with half of all pages taking over 24 s. The server was waiting on the database, not working: it allowed only 10 database connections at once, and each trip to the database takes about 76 ms from this PC. I raised the limit to 40, which nearly tripled throughput.
- **A failed Developer mode run counted as finished.** If a run stopped on a bad key, a Speed Race or VS game still saved its partial numbers to the Leaderboard. It now saves nothing.
- **Three badges could never be earned.** Right Tool, Trickster and Live Wire showed on Profile, but nothing awarded them. They now work:
  - **Right Tool:** your first level 6 sort, checked on the server.
  - **Trickster:** all 6 level 8 guesses right, as you chose.
  - **Live Wire:** your first finished live run, plus 50 XP.
  - **Descriptions:** all six badges whose text was wrong or vague now say exactly what earns them. Gamer now names its four games, because the Games page doesn't say which four count.
- **Your feedback:**
  - Questions on the Predict and Check steps now sit inside their white cards. The quiz screens had the same problem and are fixed too.
  - Each level's Predict step now starts with a short note on what's about to race, on what, and how many items.
  - Vague prompts like "Who is faster?" now say what they compare.
  - I rewrote level 8's Check question and five other unclear ones. The 16 quiz questions were already clear, so I left them.
- **One e2e test failed now and then.** The Arena share test checked the deleted link too early. I proved it was the test's timing, not the app, and fixed the test; it passed 16 times in a row afterwards.
