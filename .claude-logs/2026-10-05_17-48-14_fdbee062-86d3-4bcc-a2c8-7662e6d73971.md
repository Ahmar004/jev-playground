The "target not fully met" label means the R79 goal (a page loads in under 2 s on a phone) was not reached in Lighthouse's mobile model. Step-27 itself was only asked to fix the worst 2-3 causes and record before and after numbers, and it did that.

**The numbers**

Largest contentful paint (LCP), before and after the three fixes, in Lighthouse's simulated slow phone on slow 4G:

| Page | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Home | 6.9 s | 4.2 s |
| Level 1 | 7.3 s | 6.6 s |
| VS game | 6.6 s | 4.3 s |
| Arena | 4.9 s | 4.4 s |
| Sandbox | 5.8 s | 4.4 s |

Every page improved but none is under 2 s. The level page is the worst at 6.6 s.

**Why it still misses**

- **The real page is fast.** In the same runs the unthrottled trace on this PC shows first paint at 0.37 to 0.50 s and LCP at 0.37 to 0.84 s. R79 is met in practice and only fails in Lighthouse's model.
- **The simulated slow phone is bundle-bound.** The model's 4.2 to 6.6 s comes mostly from downloading and running about 650 to 800 KB of JavaScript on slow 4G with a 4x CPU slowdown. A few small fixes cannot get under 2 s. Only a much smaller initial bundle can.
- **The remaining bundle weight:**
  - PostHog is 97 KB gzipped and imported statically in 6 files, so deferring it needs a loader like the Sentry one.
  - Zod is 64 KB gzipped and shared by every page, probably through the Keys panel and provider modules.
  - Motion and Radix also load up front.
- **The level page has an extra delay.** Its intro text waits for the per-user Suspense (session plus progress query), so it can't paint with the static shell. That adds a render delay of about 0.8 s. Fixing it means moving the Learn step's text into the static part of `LevelStepper`.

**What Step-27 fixed**

1. The page-enter `rise` animation faded text in from opacity 0, which delayed the LCP paint by about 0.6 s. It now slides up without fading.
2. PostHog loaded a 33 KB surveys script on every page. It is now disabled with `disable_surveys: true`.
3. The Sentry browser SDK (117 KB gzipped) now loads when the browser is idle instead of before the page is usable. The trade-off is that an error in the first second or two, before the SDK starts, is not reported.

**Next step if the number matters**

The progress notes say Step-32 should repeat the check on the live Vercel URL. The CDN, compression and the absence of a localhost round trip will change the picture. The bigger levers are deferring PostHog, trimming Zod and moving the level intro into the static shell.
